## What we learned

- **With the same resources the CNN loses badly:** 12.4 test apples (11.8–13.2) against 31.5 (31.0–31.9) for the MLP,
  each with one CPU thread. Convolutions over a 19×19 picture are about seven times slower per step on one thread, so the
  CNN got about 95 thousand env steps in 10 minutes against about 630 thousand.
- **Given 4 threads it gets closer, not level:** 26.1 (25.1–27.7), still clearly below the MLP; the ranges don't overlap.
  It managed about 150–200 thousand steps.
- **Per env step the two learn at the same rate:** at 140 thousand steps the CNN's training mean (18.5–21.6) is where the
  MLP's was (16.9–20.4). On a 10×10 board the "picture" is small enough that an MLP can learn its patterns square by square;
  the CNN's advantage (a pattern learned once is recognised anywhere) doesn't pay for its cost here.
- **Its failures differ:** with one thread it almost never hit a wall (1% of test games) but hit itself in 99%: walls are
  the first pattern a CNN picks up.
- **When would a CNN win?** On bigger boards, where the MLP's weights grow with the square of the board and the CNN's don't,
  or with a GPU. Equal-compute comparisons (see the roadmap) would make this fairer.
