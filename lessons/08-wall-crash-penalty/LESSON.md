### Run `2026-10-15_140510` · Raise the wall-crash penalty to 30

**Raising the wall penalty to 30 cut GA's wall crashes from 78% to 47% of test games and lifted its test mean to 40.95 (baseline 36.27), but this is one seed.**

**GA:** The test mean rose to 40.95 (median 40, max 90), up from 36.27 in the baseline run with death_wall 10. Its wall share fell from 78 to 47 games, and body crashes rose from 22 to 51, so the snake now mostly dies by running into itself. Steps per apple barely moved (29 vs 28), so the snake still wanders about as much as before. Its training curve is noisy and still climbing slowly: mean_last_100 went from 1.4 at minute 3 to 15.3 at minute 10, with a plateau around 11 to 16 in the last few minutes. Training ran at about 47k steps/s with 28M env steps total, well below the 66M of the baseline run in the same 10 minutes, so the two GA runs differ in throughput as well as in reward.

**DQN** (deep Q-network, a gradient-based learner that estimates the value of each move): the test mean was 36.54 (baseline 37.56), a change of about 1 point. Its wall crashes fell from 12 to 2 games, while body crashes stayed dominant (98 of 100). Its training mean_last_100 was 21.7, and it reached only 279k env steps in 10 minutes (about 450 steps/s on one core, versus 810k in the baseline), so it saw far less data.

**Why:** The wall penalty acts as a price on one kind of death. Once hitting a wall costs 30 instead of 10, selection favours networks that keep away from walls. Those networks do not become better at avoiding their own tails, so the deaths move to the body (51 body crashes, up from 22). The gain in test mean (+4.7) is consistent with fewer wall deaths, but the steps-per-apple figure is unchanged, so the GA is not navigating more efficiently; it is dying later. Rank-based selection only uses the ordering of games, so the penalty shifts which networks rank highest rather than changing the size of the reward signal.

The 4.7-point gain is one seed. The lab's own baseline and this run also differ in GA throughput (28M vs 66M env steps), which adds noise. The effect is worth replicating before anyone treats it as real. DQN's small change is expected: it was already dying mostly by body, and the wall penalty mainly affects how the snake trades off walls against its body.

**What we learned:** Changing one penalty changes which way the agent dies, not just how often it dies: the crash moved from walls to the snake's own body. Always check the crash breakdown and steps per apple along with the score, and replicate a gain on another seed before believing it.

**Prediction check:** Partly held. The wall share fell below 60% (47%) as predicted, and DQN changed little (36.54 vs 37.56). The GA test mean did not stay within 3 points of 36.3; it rose to 40.95, a 4.7-point change.
