### Run `2026-10-15_071211` · Raise the death penalty to 30

**Raising the death penalty from 10 to 30 cut DQN's body crashes from 69% to 56% (prediction held), but its test mean fell from 27.1 to 24.9 because the crashes moved to walls.**

**DQN** (the value-learning agent, which learns the future reward of each move) scored a test mean of **24.9** (median 25, best 46) over 100 fixed-seed games. Its test games ended in a body crash 56% of the time and a wall crash 44%, against 69% body and 31% wall in run 04. It took 219 steps per game and 9 steps per apple, the same efficiency as before. Its training curve climbed to about 20–22 by minute 2.75 and then flattened. The final training mean of 18.75 is lower because it includes random exploration moves. DQN used 449k environment steps in 5 minutes, about 1,500 steps per second on one core.

**GA** (the evolution agent, which breeds networks by mutation and selection, with no gradients) scored a test mean of **32.8** (median 33, best 38). It ended 64% of games in a body crash and 36% in a wall crash, up from 11% wall in run 04. Its training mean rose steadily to about 26 by minute 3 and then levelled off. It ran 63.8 million steps in 5 minutes, about 140 times DQN's step count, at roughly 200k steps per second across 11 cores.

**Why:** The penalty applies to every crash, whether into the body or a wall, so it cannot teach DQN which crash to avoid. The stronger penalty made DQN crash into walls more often: wall crashes went from 31 to 44 of 100 games. That fits the result: the snake's failure mode changed, and its score did not rise. The 2.2-point drop in DQN's score is within the spread of the other DQN rays runs (25.8 to 27.1), so one seed cannot show it is a real loss. For the GA, the +7.8 change (25.1 to 32.8) is also within the spread of the other 5–10 minute GA runs (25 to 34). It is probably luck in which population evolved, not an effect of the penalty. Both agents share the same observation, so the death penalty could also be exposing a limit of the rays view: with 4 squares of look-ahead, the snake may see its body only when it is already close.

**What we learned:** A penalty tells the agent that a crash is bad, not where or why it happened, so a stronger penalty can shift crashes to another place without improving the score. Always check the score and how games end together, not just the one crash share the prediction targeted.

**Prediction check:** Held on both numbers: DQN's body share fell to 56% (below 60%) and its test mean of 24.9 landed inside the 24 to 30 band, at the low edge, though the score did not rise as the lesson hoped.
